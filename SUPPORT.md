# Support

Use the channel that best fits your request:

- For general questions and usage help, use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/LaurentMaxhuni/OpenLearn/discussions).
- For reproducible defects, open a [Bug report](https://github.com/LaurentMaxhuni/OpenLearn/issues/new?template=bug_report.md).
- For ideas and proposed capabilities, open a [Feature request](https://github.com/LaurentMaxhuni/OpenLearn/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) or use Discussions for early exploration.
- For vulnerabilities, follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

When asking for help or reporting a problem, include context, expected behavior, actual behavior, and relevant logs. Do not share credentials or personal data.
